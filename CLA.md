## Why this exists

The Apache License 2.0 that governs this project grants **the project** rights
in your contribution. It does not grant the maintainer the right to offer that
contribution under different terms later.

That distinction matters here. Parts of this ecosystem may be offered under a
commercial licence in future, alongside the Apache-2.0 release. Without this
Agreement, doing so would require locating and obtaining fresh permission from
every past contributor — which becomes impractical quickly, and can leave a
project permanently unable to change its own licensing.

everything you write, and you remain free to use your contribution anywhere

## How to sign

Open a pull request. An automated check will ask you to agree to this document,
and records your agreement against your GitHub account. You sign once; it
applies to every repository in the project and to all your future contributions.

If the automated check is not yet configured, add the following as a comment on
your pull request:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement and I hereby agree to its terms
> for all my present and future contributions to this project.
